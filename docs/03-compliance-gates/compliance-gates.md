\# Compliance Gates



\## Objective



Compliance Gates ensure that every software release meets security, quality, and regulatory requirements before deployment.



\---



\# Why Compliance Gates?



Banking applications must satisfy strict regulatory and security standards. Compliance Gates prevent non-compliant software from reaching production.
